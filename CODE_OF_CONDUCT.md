# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in PoXter welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while staying accountable for impact.
- Give and receive feedback professionally.
- Keep discussions focused on improving the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized attention or content in project spaces.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or bad-faith participation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or limit participation when behavior harms the project or community. Reports will be reviewed fairly and with appropriate confidentiality.
